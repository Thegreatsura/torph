A word no longer character-morphs across a word that is staying put

`Copy Address` → `Address Copied` shares `Cop` between the two verbs, so the
diff paired them and carried three characters the width of the value, past the
`Address` holding still between them. A resemblance now only earns a character
morph when both words sit in the same gap between surviving words; `Copy` exits
and `Copied` enters, as it did before word pairing existed.
